- If you have a complaint about content in the app, about your data, or about the service, you can contact our Grievance Officer. Their name and contact details are shown on the Contact & Grievance screen.
- You can send a grievance from the app (Settings → Contact & Grievance) without an account. You get a reference number like GR-2026-000123.
- Leaving an e-mail or phone number is optional; we need it only if you want a reply. It is stored encrypted.
- We acknowledge every grievance within 24 hours and aim to resolve it within 72 hours. Complaints about content follow the timelines in our content policy.
- To report a specific post, use Report in the post's "…" menu — it reaches us faster.
- If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may approach the authorities named under Indian law (for example, the Grievance Appellate Committee for decisions about content).
