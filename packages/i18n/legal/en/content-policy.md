- Masjid Connect is for a masjid's own news: prayer and jamaat timings, special dates, notices, dua requests, donation campaigns, chanda totals, bayans and verified Hadith, Ayah and Dua texts.
- Not allowed: hateful, insulting or abusive content; anything political or that could cause conflict between people or communities; false or misleading information; fraud or fake donation requests.
- Not allowed: private information about someone without their permission (phone numbers, addresses, health details). A dua request names a person only with their family's permission.
- Not allowed: sexual or violent content, pretending to be another masjid or person, and content the masjid has no right to use (photos, videos, music).
- Religious texts (Hadith, Ayah, Dua) come only from the app's verified library; admins do not type their own.
- Anyone can report a post. Reports about intimate images or impersonation are handled within 2 hours; other reports within 36 hours.
- When we remove a post, it disappears for everyone at once. It is kept privately for 180 days, as Indian law requires, and then deleted. The masjid's admins are told which rule it broke.
- Masjids or admins that break these rules again may be suspended. Orders from authorities are acted on within the time the law sets.
- Questions or appeals: contact our Grievance Officer (Settings → Contact & Grievance).
