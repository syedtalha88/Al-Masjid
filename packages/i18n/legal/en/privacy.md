- Masjid Connect is a free app that keeps you connected to your local masjids. It has no accounts and no advertising.
- On our server we keep only: a random app ID (with a secret that only your phone knows), your language, your brother/sister choice, the version of this notice you accepted, the masjids you follow and which of them you muted, and the day the app was last used.
- If you turn on notifications, we also keep the notification address your phone's push service gives the app, so we can send you your masjids' updates.
- We never collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, location or IP address. Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your phone.
- When you say Ameen on a dua request, we record that this app ID said it, so it counts once. The masjid sees only the total.
- We use this information only to show your masjids' timings and updates in your language and to send the notifications you ask for.
- If the app is not used for 180 days, your app ID and the masjids you follow are deleted automatically.
- Settings → My data shows what we keep about this app. Settings → Clear all data deletes it from our server and from this phone at once.
- Our service providers: hosting in India (our server in Mumbai and MongoDB Atlas, Mumbai), Cloudflare (network delivery and bot protection), and — only if you turn on notifications — your phone's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla).
- For any question or complaint, contact our Grievance Officer (Settings → Contact & Grievance). We acknowledge within 24 hours.
- If this notice changes, the app will ask you to read it again.
