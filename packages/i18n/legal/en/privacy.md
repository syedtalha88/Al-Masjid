- Masjid Connect is a free app that keeps you connected to your local masjids. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.
- What we keep about this app: a random app ID with a secret that only your phone knows, your language, your brother/sister choice, the version of this notice you accepted, the masjids you follow and which of them you muted, and the day the app was last used.
- If you turn on notifications, we also keep the notification address your phone's push service gives the app, so we can send you your masjids' updates.
- If you report a post, we keep the report (reason and optional details) and which app ID sent it, so one phone cannot report the same post many times. The masjid never sees who reported.
- If you send a grievance, we keep what you wrote and its reference number. A contact (e-mail or phone) is optional; it is stored encrypted and only our Grievance Officer can read it.
- We never collect your name, phone number, e-mail address, contacts, location or IP address in the app's records. Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your phone; your location never leaves it.
- My zakat (in Settings) is a private list kept only on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone; Clear all data, or changing to a new phone, removes it.
- When you say Ameen on a dua request, we record that this app ID said it, so it counts once. The masjid sees only the total.
- Masjid admins (imams, committee members) have an admin account: their name, an encrypted phone number, their passkey's public key (fingerprint or face data never leaves their phone), their sessions and what they published.
- We use this information only to show your masjids' timings and updates in your language, to send the notifications you ask for, to keep the service safe from misuse, and to meet our legal duties.
- How long we keep it: an app ID and its follows are deleted after 180 days without use; dead notification addresses after 30 days; reports 1 year after they are closed; grievances 3 years after they are resolved; removed posts are kept privately for 180 days, as Indian law requires, and then deleted; the security log 2 years.
- Our service providers: our server host (servers in Mumbai, India); MongoDB Atlas (database, Mumbai, India); Cloudflare (network delivery, security and bot checks, worldwide); Cloudinary (photos and voice notes the masjids publish, and encrypted legal documents — region to be confirmed); Sentry (technical error reports without your personal data — region to be confirmed); YouTube (only when you tap Play on a bayan video); and, only if you turn on notifications, your phone's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla).
- Your rights: Settings → My data shows what we keep about this app; Settings → Clear all data deletes it from our server and this phone at once. You can also ask our Grievance Officer to see, correct or delete your data, or complain about how we handle it.
- Grievance Officer: details are in Settings → Contact & Grievance. We acknowledge within 24 hours and aim to resolve within 72 hours.
- Children: the app asks no one their age and collects no identity, so it holds no data that identifies a child.
- We never sell your data, show ads, or build profiles.
- If this notice changes, the app will show it to you again before you continue, and once a year as a reminder.
